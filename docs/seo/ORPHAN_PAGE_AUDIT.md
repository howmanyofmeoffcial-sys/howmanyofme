## 3. Detailed Root Causes & Fix Actions

### Issue 1: 448 Orphan Full Name Profiles (`/people/...`)
* **Root Cause:** In `src/lib/fullNames/data.ts`, `getRelatedFullNames` artificially sliced candidate full names to 4 items per first name and 4 items per surname. Because there are 700 canonical full names across 583 first names and 50 surnames, any full name combination beyond the 4th slot was omitted from both its parent first-name profile and its surname profile.
* **Fix Action:** Refactored `getRelatedFullNames` to dynamically deliver all associated full-name combinations (up to 20 per first-name profile and up to 40 per surname profile).
* **Result:** 100% of all 700 full-name pages now have 2+ direct incoming links (1 from `/name/[First]` and 1 from `/last-name/[Last]`).

### Issue 2: 20 Orphan Head-to-Head Comparison Pages (`/name-comparison/...`)
* **Root Cause:** Dedicated comparison pages (such as `/name-comparison/liam-vs-noah` and `/name-comparison/emma-vs-olivia`) were generated as static SSG routes but were not linked from the main `/tools/name-comparison` hub or from the respective `/name/[name]` profiles.
* **Fix Action:**
  1. Added a categorized "Featured Side-by-Side Name Comparisons" section on [`/tools/name-comparison`](file:///Users/riponchakma/Downloads/Howmanyofme/src/pages/tools/name-comparison.astro) linking all 20 pairs.
  2. Added a contextual "Head-to-Head Name Comparisons" section on [`/name/[name]`](file:///Users/riponchakma/Downloads/Howmanyofme/src/pages/name/%5Bname%5D.astro) for every name in a comparison pair.
* **Result:** All 20 comparison pages now have incoming links from high-authority hubs.

### Issue 3: 1 Orphan Research Report (`/research/name-popularity-by-decade`)
* **Root Cause:** The 145-year demographic study was published at `/research/name-popularity-by-decade` but was missing a prominent entry link from `/data`.
* **Fix Action:** Added a dedicated "Featured Demographic Study" card on [`/data`](file:///Users/riponchakma/Downloads/Howmanyofme/src/pages/data/index.astro) with a direct link.
* **Result:** Fully connected to the Open Data cluster.

### Issue 4: 1 Orphan Legal Route (`/disclaimer`)
* **Root Cause:** `/disclaimer` existed in the route table but was omitted from the footer navigation list.
* **Fix Action:** Added `Disclaimer` link to [`src/components/SiteFooter.astro`](file:///Users/riponchakma/Downloads/Howmanyofme/src/components/SiteFooter.astro).
* **Result:** Accessible sitewide across every page footer.
