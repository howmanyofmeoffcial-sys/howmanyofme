# Authority, Linkable Assets & Digital PR

## 1. Strategy Overview
HowManyOfMe.co builds long-term topical authority exclusively through high-utility data assets, academic citations, transparent government methodologies, and embeddable badges—strictly rejecting paid or manipulative link schemes.

---

## 2. Linkable Core Assets

### A. Open Data Hub (`/data`)
- **URL**: `https://howmanyofme.co/data`
- **Features**: Downloadable JSON and CSV dataset summaries of top 100 first names and surnames.
- **Target Audience**: Researchers, journalists, genealogists, and data scientists seeking verified public records.

### B. Flagship Research Reports (`/research/name-popularity-by-decade`)
- **URL**: `https://howmanyofme.co/research/name-popularity-by-decade`
- **Features**: Longitudinal statistical study analyzing baby name shifts from 1880 through 2024 across 145 cohorts.

### C. Reusable Citation & Provenance Blocks
- Built via `src/components/CitationBlock.astro` and interactive `src/islands/CopyCitationButton.tsx`.
- Provides pre-formatted APA, MLA, and Chicago academic citation snippets to encourage natural editorial attribution.

### D. Lightweight Embed Badges (`/embed/name/[name]`)
- Self-contained, responsive demographic summary card for bloggers and webmasters to embed on external sites.
- Configured with `robots: noindex, nofollow` to prevent iframe duplication while generating natural brand attribution backlinks.

---

## 3. Strict Link-Building Policy
- ❌ **Prohibited**: Private Blog Networks (PBNs), paid link placements, automated comment spam, directory submissions, reciprocal link rings.
- ✅ **Approved**: Open Data releases, research citations, press releases on government dataset updates, educational resource roundups.
