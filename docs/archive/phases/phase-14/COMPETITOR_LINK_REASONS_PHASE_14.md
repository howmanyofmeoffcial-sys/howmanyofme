# Phase 14 — Analysis of Why Competitor Pages Earn Backlinks
## Project: HowManyOfMe.co

Date: August 14, 2026

---

## 1. Primary Citation Drivers Observed in SERP Competitors

1. **Novelty & Calculator Utility**: Writers and forum posters cite the "How Many People Have My Name" calculator for personal amusement, character naming, and fiction writing.
2. **Genealogical Reference**: Family history bloggers link to specific given-name and surname distribution numbers when investigating ancestral prevalence.
3. **Journalism & Data Fact-Checking**: News outlets covering unusual names or political figures link to name frequency statistics as background context.
4. **Classroom & Educational Guides**: Math, sociology, and statistics curricula reference population frequency modeling as examples of probability in real life.

---

## 2. Our Qualitative Advantage
Instead of copying competitor link profiles, HowManyOfMe.co will capture these citation vectors through **evidence-grounded datasets, transparent formulas, downloadable CSV summaries, and clean attribution blocks**.
