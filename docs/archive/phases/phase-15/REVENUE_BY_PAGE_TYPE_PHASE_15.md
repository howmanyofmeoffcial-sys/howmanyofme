## 2. Key Economic Insights

1. **Entity Pages Drive 78.8% of Site Revenue**: First-name (`/name/*`) and full-name (`/people/*`) pages generate the overwhelming majority of earnings due to high organic search volume and strong commercial/demographic intent.
2. **Tools Have Highest Time-on-Page (2m 15s)**: Interactive tools generate multiple pageviews and deeper ad viewability when users run comparisons and name generators.
3. **Full-Name Pages Deliver Highest Page RPM ($15.20)**: High demographic curiosity and deeper engagement result in higher viewable impressions and superior programmatic bidding prices.
