## 2. CTR Optimization Rules

1. **Include Verified Numbers**: Meta descriptions should feature numbers (e.g. `~31,000 living people in the U.S.`, `Rank #1`).
2. **Eliminate Wordiness**: Frontload the entity name and the primary question within the first 60 characters.
3. **Signal Official Freshness**: Include `SSA 1880–2024` and `Census 2020` authority badges in descriptions.
