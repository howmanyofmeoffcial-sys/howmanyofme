## 3. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) & GEO Readability

Every name page follows an **Answer-First Structure**:
1. **Primary Query Answer**: The immediate paragraph following the `H1` answers *"How many people have the name [Name]?"* with estimated living bearers, rank, and 1-in-X frequency.
2. **Clear Question Headings**:
   - `About the Name [Name]`
   - `Historical Popularity & Decade Trends of [Name]`
   - `Regional & Geographic Distribution of [Name]`
   - `Gender Distribution & Cultural Background`
   - `Frequently Asked Questions About [Name]`
3. **Structured Data Parity**: `FAQPage`, `WebPage` (with `about` Thing entity), and `BreadcrumbList` mirror the visible server-rendered text exactly.
