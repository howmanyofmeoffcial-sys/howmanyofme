## 2. Competitive Winning Strategy

1. **Information Density**: Deliver verified historical and decennial census facts in the top 30% of viewport without intrusive ad walls.
2. **Schema Authority**: Leverage clean `WebPage`, `FAQPage`, and `BreadcrumbList` schema to dominate rich SERP features.
3. **Internal Discovery Depth**: Every full-name page links to first names, and first-name pages link to top full-name combinations, creating a dense, crawlable topical cluster.
