## 2. Intent-Based Monetization Rules

1. **Never Disrupt Answer Intent**: Users searching for an exact frequency count must see the number instantly in the hero viewport without layout shift or interstitial barriers.
2. **Contextual Continuity**: After viewing a first name, the most logical next action is checking that first name paired with a common surname (`/people/[first-last]`) or comparing with a peer name (`/tools/name-comparison`).
3. **No Irrelevant Monetization**: Academic and open data visitors should see citable datasets and download links, rather than aggressive consumer popups.
