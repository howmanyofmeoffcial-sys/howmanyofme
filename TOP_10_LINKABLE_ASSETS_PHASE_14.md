8. **Surname Prevalence & Origin Directory (Planned Phase 15)**: Dedicated `/last-name/*` entity hub for Census surnames. *Priority: Tier B*.

10. **Genealogical Name Matcher Tool (Planned Phase 15)**: Historical era matcher for fiction writers and family historians. *Priority: Tier C*.
